BANK! Dice Party Game

A fast, loud party version of the classic BANK dice game. One screen keeps score; everyone else joins with a code and taps BANK from their own phone.

What it is

BANK! is played with two real dice (or the app's virtual dice). Every roll grows a shared pot, the BANK. Players decide when to bank the pot into their own score before someone rolls a seven and wipes it out. The app keeps score, tracks rounds, and lets every player bank from their phone.

Host a table

  1. On the scorekeeper's device, tap Host a table. A 4-digit table code appears.
  2. Choose 10, 15, or 20 rounds, and wait for players to join.
  3. When at least two players have joined, tap Start the game.
  4. Enter each roll on the pad (or use virtual dice). The pot, round, and scores update on every phone.

Join a table

  1. Tap Join with a code.
  2. Enter the 4-digit code from the host's screen and the name you want shown at the table.
  3. When the pot looks good, tap BANK to take it before someone rolls a 7.

One device only

No phones to pass around? Tap This device only, add everyone's names, and let one person tap names on the scoreboard as players bank. Past games played this way are kept on that device only.

Quick rules

  • The first three rolls of each round are safe: a seven adds 70 and doesn't end the round, and doubles don't double the pot.
  • From the fourth roll on, a seven busts the pot and ends the round. Doubles (or a 2 or 12) double the whole BANK.
  • Each player can bank once per round, then sits out until the next round.
  • Whoever has banked the most points after the last round wins.

The full rules are in the app under Rules, and they're also shown if you open the app without an internet connection.

Troubleshooting

“You're offline right now”

Hosting and joining need an internet connection. Check Wi-Fi or cellular data and tap Retry.

A player can't join

Make sure they typed the exact 4-digit code from the host's screen and that the table hasn't been closed. Names can be 1 to 18 characters.

Scores look out of sync

Every phone follows the host's table. If one phone falls behind, close and reopen the app. It reconnects to the same table automatically.
