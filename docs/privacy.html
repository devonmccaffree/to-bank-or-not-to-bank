BANK! Dice Party Game

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026.

This policy explains what the BANK! Dice Party Game app and its website at bankgame.grok.me (together, “the app”) collect and why. The short version: there are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. The app only keeps what it needs to run a game.

What the app collects

When you host or join a table

To let several phones share one game, the app stores the following on its server:

  • Table code: a random 4-digit code that identifies the table.
  • Player names: the name each player types when joining (1 to 18 characters). Use a nickname if you like; it doesn't need to be your real name.
  • Seat order and random access tokens: these let the host and each player reconnect to their own seat. They aren't linked to your identity or your device.
  • Game state: rolls, the pot, round number, scores, who banked, and a short undo history.

Other players at the same table can see the names and scores. When a code is reused for a new table, the old table and its player list are deleted. The host can also close a table. If you want a table's data removed sooner, email me and I'll delete it.

Stored only on your device

The app uses your device's local storage to save:

  • A game in progress, the names entered on the setup screen, your round choice, and whether sound is muted.
  • Up to 20 past games played on that device (player names, scores, and winners).
  • Your current table code and access token, so you can rejoin if the app closes.

This information isn't sent to the server unless you're playing a hosted table as described above. Deleting the app removes it.

What the app does not collect

No accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera access, advertising identifiers, or payment information. The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and doesn't sell or share personal data for advertising.

Service providers

The game runs on third-party infrastructure. These providers process network requests on my behalf, and like most web services they may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, device and browser type, and request time) for security and reliability:

ProviderPurpose
VercelHosts the game's web app and server.
NeonManaged Postgres database that stores hosted tables (the data listed above).
CloudflareNetwork delivery and security for bankgame.grok.me. It may set a short-lived security cookie (__cf_bm) used for bot protection, not tracking.
Google FontsServes the app's typefaces. Your device requests font files from Google, which receives your IP address.
Grok (xAI)The platform the app was built and deployed with. The page loads a small script from grok.com that checks whether to show a “Created with Grok” link.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them. Player names are free text, so please don't enter anything more identifying than a first name or nickname.

Your choices

  • Play in This device only mode to keep everything on your device.
  • Delete the app to clear everything stored on your device.
  • Email me to ask about or delete hosted table data.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, I'll update this page and its effective date.
